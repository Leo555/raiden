Raiden Storm - Free Online Vertical Shooter (HTML5 Arcade Shmup)

Raiden Storm is a free, browser-based vertical scrolling shoot 'em up (shmup) inspired by the classic Raiden arcade series. Pilot your fighter through 6 increasingly challenging sectors, take down epic bosses, upgrade your weapons by collecting power-ups, and chain combos for high scores. No download, no signup - just instant arcade action.

Game features

  • Classic top-down vertical shooter gameplay (shmup, bullet hell)
  • 6 unique boss battles with multi-phase attack patterns
  • Weapon upgrades, homing missiles, screen-clearing nukes, super shield
  • Combo and grazing system rewarding skilled play
  • Endless mode after the main campaign
  • Smooth 60 FPS HTML5 Canvas rendering, runs on desktop and mobile
  • Procedural Web Audio music and sound effects
  • Free, no installation, no ads, no signup

How to play

Use WASD or Arrow Keys to move your ship, or hold a mouse / touch to drag it. Press SPACE to launch a screen-clearing bomb and P to pause. Collect the P power-up to level up your main weapon, and grab rare items like the Nuke (N) or Super Shield (X) for emergencies.
